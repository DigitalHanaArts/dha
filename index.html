01 / The Studio

Technology is material.

Digital Hana Arts is an experimental computational creativity studio exploring the space between technology and artistic expression.

We develop software, visual systems, machine-learning experiments, data-driven artwork and blockchain-oriented digital assets.

Our central question is simple: What happens when computation becomes a creative medium?
